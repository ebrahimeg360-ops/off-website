How it works

Three steps. No app to argue with.

Everything happens on your own phone — OFF just holds the switch.

Choose what goes quiet

Pick the apps that pull you in. Save it as a block you can reuse whenever you need it.

Tap your phone to it

One tap starts the session. No app to open, nothing to configure in the moment.

Walk back to end it

Ending a session means going to the object. The short walk is usually enough.

Schedules that run themselves

Set a window — bedtime, study hours, dinner — and OFF applies and lifts the block automatically, no tapping required.

Nothing leaves your phone

Your app choices and usage stay on the device. We only ever see the email you sign in with.

Why the usual fixes stop working

Every other option keeps the key in your pocket.

Questions

Everything people ask

Can I get around it?

If you're determined, yes — it's friction, not a lock. That's deliberate. It makes the easy path the one you actually chose, while leaving a real path available for genuine emergencies.

What if I need a blocked app urgently?

The app holds a small number of emergency unlocks that end a session immediately. They're limited on purpose, so they stay for real emergencies.

Does it see what I do on my phone?

No. Which apps you block and how you use your phone stay on the phone. We only store the email address you sign in with.

Does it need charging or pairing?

Neither. There's no battery and no Bluetooth. Your phone powers the tag for the instant it reads it, the same way a contactless card works.

Can two people share one?

Yes. Any phone with the app can use the same device, and each person's blocks stay on their own phone.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet. OFF is iPhone-only today.
