FAQ

Everything people ask

How does OFF actually block apps?

You choose which apps to block inside the free OFF app. Tapping your phone to the device switches that block on using Apple's built-in Screen Time system. Tap again to switch it off.

Does it need charging or Bluetooth?

No. The device has no battery and no electronics to pair. It works by a simple tap, so there is nothing to charge or connect.

Which phones does it work with?

iPhone XR and newer, running a recent version of iOS. An Android version is planned.

Can I still get calls and messages?

Yes. Only the apps you choose are paused. Calls, messages and everything else keep working normally.
