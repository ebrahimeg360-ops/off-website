Getting started

  1. Download OFF from the App Store and sign in with your email.

  2. Allow Screen Time access when the app asks. Blocking cannot work without it.

  3. Open Blocks, choose the apps you want gone, and save.

  4. Hold the top of your iPhone against the OFF device. The session starts.

  5. Tap the device again whenever you want your apps back.

Troubleshooting
Nothing happens when I tap the device

Hold the very top edge of the back of your iPhone against the centre of the device and keep it still for a second. NFC on iPhone is read from the top, not the middle. Make sure the screen is on and unlocked, and that your case isn't unusually thick or metal.

It says this isn't a registered OFF tag

Only genuine OFF devices work with the app. If you're tapping a device you bought from us and see this, email us the order details and we'll sort it out.

Apps aren't actually being blocked

Open Settings inside the app and check that Screen Time access says granted. If it says denied, go to the iPhone's Settings app, then Screen Time, and allow OFF. Also confirm your block has apps selected — a block with nothing chosen does nothing.

I didn't get my sign-in code

Check your spam folder first. Codes expire after a short time, so request a fresh one if it's been a while. If nothing arrives at all, email us from the address you're trying to use.

I'm stuck in a session and can't find my device

Open the app, go to Settings, and use an emergency unlock. It ends the session immediately. Use them sparingly — there's a limited number.

My schedule didn't start

Scheduled windows have to be at least 15 minutes long — that's an Apple requirement. Also check the schedule is enabled and has a block selected that contains apps.

I signed out and lost my blocks

That's intended. Signing out clears this device's blocks, schedules, and history so the next person starts fresh. Your blocks aren't stored on our servers.
